Privacy Policy

StellarOps Mission Lab · NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 project by Team StellarOps · Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short: StellarOps Mission Lab is a free educational game. Signing in is optional. We only keep what the game needs to work: your name, your level progress, and what you type or choose inside a game room. We never sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone for marketing.

1. What we collect

  • If you sign in with Google: your name, your email address and a Google account ID, given to us by Google. We use them only to recognize you and save which levels you finished.
  • Level progress: which levels you finished and how many stars you got.
  • Online team rooms: the name you type, the role you play, your choices in the game, your notes on the team board, your chat messages, and your votes. Everyone in the same room can see these.
  • On your own device: the browser keeps your settings and progress in local storage, so the game remembers them. This stays on your device.

We do not collect your password, contacts, location, camera, microphone, or payment details.

2. Why we use it

  • To run online team games (rooms, chat, votes).
  • To save your finished levels so they follow you to other devices when you sign in.
  • To unlock the next level for a team when everyone has finished the level before.

3. Where it is stored

Game data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase. Google sign-in is handled by Google and Supabase; we never see your Google password. The game website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

4. Who can see it

  • Players in the same online room can see your name, role, choices, notes, chat and votes in that room.
  • Your email address and level progress are private to your account.
  • The StellarOps team can see the database to keep the game working and to fix problems.

5. How long we keep it

This is a hackathon project. We keep game data only as long as the project runs, and we may delete old rooms and data at any time.

6. Your choices

  • You can play without signing in.
  • You can sign out at any time from the game's home page.
  • You can ask us to delete your account data. Open an issue on the project page below and write "delete my data", without posting your email. We will reply there and delete it.
  • You can remove the game's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Children

The game is made for students. We collect as little as possible. Younger students can play without signing in, or with a parent's or teacher's help.

8. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. Questions: open an issue on the project page, github.com/mohamdkhaldd/shopify-mcp-v1/issues.
