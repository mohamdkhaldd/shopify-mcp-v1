Physics in this game is simplified for learning: Tsiolkovsky rocket equation (chemical engines, Isp 320 s), sunlight of 300 W per m² of panel at Earth's distance falling with distance squared, batteries sized for shadow time. Real-mission facts come from NASA and ESA mission pages. Textures: NASA, and the three.js example textures (NASA Blue Marble imagery). Portraits are AI-generated, fictional people. References: NASA Planetary Fact Sheets · NASA Science Missions · NASA Small Spacecraft Technology.
